Stock Classification Guide

Two separate readings used in every stock list below
Trend / leadership

Measures whether the stock is participating in a durable trend using relative strength, moving averages, RSI, and Point & Figure. Leader means strong technical alignment; it is not an instruction to hold or buy. Partial means shorter price history limits long-term confirmation.

Risk flag

Separately highlights weak structure, bearish P&F, or a low technical score. A stock can rise strongly today and still show Risk review because a one-day gain does not by itself repair the broader trend.

Read the columns together: Day move describes what happened today. Trend / leadership describes the broader technical position. Risk flag identifies where closer review is warranted.

Technical Reading Guide

What the technical layer is measuring
Relative Strength

Compares the stock with Nifty Midcap. Positive RS vs 50D means the stock is leading the broader market.

RSI 14

Momentum gauge. 40-60 is often a healthy reset, above 70 can be extended, below 30 can be oversold.

Moving Averages

50DMA tracks medium-term trend. 200DMA tracks long-term structure. Above both is usually stronger.

Point & Figure

Filters noise and highlights breakouts or breakdowns. It is a structure check, not a standalone signal.

Signal Agreement

Summarises whether trend, relative strength, momentum, and P&F point in the same direction. It is evidence confluence, not a calibrated probability or instruction.

Review Priority Guide

How to read the High, Medium, and Low labels used in All Holdings
High Review first

Triggered when a holding is at least 10% of the portfolio, moves at least 5% in one day, or its market ticker needs confirmation.

Medium Review next

Triggered when a holding is 5% to under 10% of the portfolio, or moves 2.5% to under 5% in one day, provided no High trigger applies.

Low Routine monitoring

No High or Medium review trigger is active. The holding remains visible for normal portfolio monitoring and can still be opened for full detail.

How to use it: Priority highlights where discussion may be most useful. It does not predict direction and does not recommend a transaction.
